unit.cancelFormFor (outOfFormSpell, treatOutSpellAsFree, emergency)
Leave the current shapeshift form to cast a spell, when a player would: not while stealthed, not while tanking in a bear form, and only with the mana to cast it and shift back. In an emergency it leaves regardless. Shows "Cancel Form []" in profile debug once, when the cancel is sent. In combat, until a form is up again, casts other than that spell leave the mana to shift back into the form left.

Parameters:

  • outOfFormSpell Spell to cast once out of form (id/name/spell table).
  • treatOutSpellAsFree If true, that spell costs nothing this time (e.g. Clearcasting).
  • emergency If true, leave form regardless (e.g. an emergency heal).

Returns:

    boolean True while the form is being left: cancelled now, or a cancel still on its way (keep waiting rather than acting as if still in form).
unit.canLeaveFormFor (outOfFormSpell, treatOutSpellAsFree, emergency)
Whether unit.cancelFormFor would leave the current form for this spell now (same rules), without leaving it: for steps that come before the spell, such as a stun ahead of a heal. True when not in a form.

Parameters:

  • outOfFormSpell Spell to cast once out of form (id/name/spell table).
  • treatOutSpellAsFree If true, that spell costs nothing this time (e.g. Clearcasting).
  • emergency If true, the mana to shift back isn't needed.

Returns:

  1. boolean
  2. string why not, when false
